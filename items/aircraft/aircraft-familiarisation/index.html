Aircraft Familiarisation Training

Welcome, trainee. This module introduces the main parts of a fighter jet in plain English. No aviation background is needed. You can open them in any order; working through them from 1 to 3 is recommended.

  • Module 2: Fuselage and Wing Structure
  • Module 3: Engine and Empennage
