Cut the Thrust: Clean vs Defective Aircraft

Set the aircraft's Hangar Condition (clean, or with defects), reduce the thrust, choose how the aircraft responds, and watch what happens. Turn on Compare mode to fly a clean aircraft beside the defective one.

The four forces

Thrust and drag arrows are drawn larger than lift and weight so they are easy to compare. Grey dotted arrows show the clean aircraft at the same speed, for comparison. Numbered red markers are the defects switched on in the Hangar Condition panel.

    Forces in the hover

    The drag arrow is the air resistance the spinning blades must push through (rotor drag); it is drawn larger so changes are easy to see. The tail rotor pushes sideways (out of the page) to stop the main rotor's torque turning the helicopter: see the top view. Height is not drawn to scale. Numbered red markers are the defects switched on in the Hangar Condition panel.
