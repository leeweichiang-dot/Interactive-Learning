Pitot-Static Flight Instruments

How air pressure becomes the numbers on the cockpit instruments. This is a generic training cockpit, not a real aircraft.

    1. System Overview

    An aircraft cannot feel how fast or how high it is. It measures air pressure and turns that into readings.

    Pitot pressure

    A forward-facing tube (the pitot tube) catches air as the aircraft moves. Faster flight gives higher pressure.

    Static pressure

    Small holes (static ports) on the side of the aircraft measure the pressure of the still air around it. This pressure falls as you climb.

    Three instruments use these pressures

    • Airspeed indicator: compares pitot and static pressure.
    • Altimeter: reads static pressure and shows height.
    • Vertical speed indicator: shows how fast static pressure is changing, so how fast you climb or descend.
